Membrane Filtration Systems

Pressure-driven membrane processes used for selective separation, concentration and purification of dairy streams.

RONFUFMFCross-flow
01 • FOUR MEMBRANES

Choose by separation level

Each membrane class has a different retention range.

Reverse Osmosis (RO)

Dense membrane mainly used for water removal and concentration.

Nanofiltration (NF)

Selective separation between RO and UF in many applications.

Ultrafiltration (UF)

Retains macromolecules such as proteins.

Microfiltration (MF)

Retains suspended particles and many microorganisms.
