Q12

97%
85%
70%
90%
Answer: (4) 90%
Explanation
Total fat in raw milk = 2000 * 0.08 = 160 kg. Total fat recovered in cream = 250 * 0.45 = 112.5 kg. Skimming Efficiency = (112.5 / 160) * 100 = 70.3%. Option 3 is 70%. [Ref: Dairy Technology Calculations]

Q29
A Carnot engine operates between 600 K (source) and 300 K (sink). What is its efficiency?
25%
50%
75%
90%
Answer: (2) 50%
Explanation
The efficiency of a Carnot engine is Efficiency = 1 - (T_sink / T_source). Here, 1 - (300 / 600) = 1 - 0.5 = 0.5. Converted to a percentage, the efficiency is exactly 50%. [Ref: Thermodynamics]

266
400
495
650
Answer: (2) 400
Explanation
Shear Force (F) = μ * A * (dv/dy). μ = 1 poise = 0.1 Pa.s. A = 1.5 m². dv = 0.4 m/s. dy = 0.00015 m. F = 0.1 * 1.5 * (0.4 / 0.00015) = 0.15 * 2666.67 = 400 N. [Ref: Fluid Mechanics]
