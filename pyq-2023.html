2023 Dairy Technology PYQ

Q1
Which type of ghee has more number of larger grains under given
granulation conditions?
(1) Cow milk ghee
(2) Buffalo milk ghee
(3) Mixed ghee
(4) Recombined milk ghee
Answer: (2) Buffalo milk ghee
Explanation
Buffalo milk fat has a higher proportion of high-melting triglycerides
(like palmitic and stearic acids) compared to cow milk fat, which naturally leads to
the formation of more numerous and larger grains during cooling.
Q2
Sterilisation temperature/time of milk is
(1) 100-108°C/8-10 min
(2) 121°C/20-25 min
(3) 110-118°C for 15-25 min
(4) 120-130°C no hold
Answer: (3) 110-118°C for 15-25 min
Explanation
Standard in-bottle sterilization of milk requires heating to 110-118°C
and holding for 15-25 minutes to ensure the destruction of all bacterial spores while
minimizing browning.
Q3
Phosphatase test is negative in
(1) Pasteurised milk
(2) Chilled milk
(3) Thermised milk
(4) Irradiated milk
Answer: (1) Pasteurised milk
Explanation
The alkaline phosphatase enzyme is naturally present in raw milk and
is completely deactivated at temperatures just slightly higher than those required to
kill Mycobacterium tuberculosis, making it the perfect indicator for successful
pasteurization.
Q4
Moisture content of khoa varieties vary as follows in decreasing order
(1) Danedar, Pindi, Dhap
(2) Dhap, Danedar, Pindi
(3) Dhap, Pindi, Danedar
(4) Pindi, Danedar, Dhap
Answer: (2) Dhap, Danedar, Pindi
Explanation
Dhap khoa has the highest moisture content (37-44%) suitable for gulab
jamun, followed by Danedar (35-40%) for kalakand, and Pindi has the lowest (31-33%)
for burfi/peda.
Q5
Sweet sensing taste buds are mainly situated at
(1) Tip of tongue
(2) Back of tongue
(3) Sides of tongue
(4) Middle of tongue
Answer: (1) Tip of tongue
Explanation
According to traditional sensory physiology maps, sweet receptors are
most concentrated and highly responsive at the very tip of the human tongue.
Q6
In electrodialysis membranes, which type of current is used?
(1) DC
(2) AC
(3) Static charges
(4) Sinusoidal
Answer: (1) DC
Explanation
Electrodialysis relies on a Direct Current (DC) field to drive cations and
anions across ion-selective membranes to demineralize whey. AC current would
simply oscillate ions back and forth.
Q7
Which among the following tastes has highest threshold value?
(1) Sweetness
(2) Saltiness
(3) Bitterness
(4) Sourness
Answer: (1) Sweetness
Explanation
A high threshold value means lower sensitivity. Humans require a
relatively high concentration of sugar (sweetness) to detect it, whereas bitterness can
be detected at microscopic trace levels (lowest threshold).
Q8
Arrange the following milk processing operations in correct sequence:
(A) Separation (B) Clarification (C) Standardisation (D) Homogenization (E)
Pasteurisation
(1) (A), (C), (B), (D), (E)
(2) (C), (B), (A), (D), (E)
(3) (B), (C), (A), (D), (E)
(4) (B), (A), (C), (D), (E)
Answer: (4) (B), (A), (C), (D), (E)
Explanation
The standard industrial fluid milk processing line sequence is: Raw
milk reception -> Clarification -> Separation -> Standardization -> Homogenization ->
Pasteurization.
Q9
Statement I: Kalakand, milk cake and sorbhaja are heat desiccated
products. Statement II: Shrikhand, lassi and kalari are fermented products.
(1) Both correct
(2) Both incorrect

Q10

Q11

(2) (A), (B) and (D) only

Q28
Butter sample analysis: 0.1 N AgNO3, Titration = 25ml, Blank = 5ml,
Weight = 5g. Salt content?
(1) 3.1%
(2) 2.5%
(3) 1.7%
(4) 2.3%
Answer: (4) 2.3%
Explanation
Salt % = (V_sample - V_blank) * N * 5.844 / Weight. (25 - 5) * 0.1 * 5.844 /
5 = 20 * 0.1 * 5.844 / 5 = 11.688 / 5 = 2.33%.

Q35
Pulse electric field treatment is mostly suitable for:
(1) Solid food materials
(2) Liquid food material with low dielectric strength
(3) Liquid food material with high dielectric strength
(4) Food paste
Answer: (3) Liquid food material with high dielectric strength
Explanation
PEF works by applying high voltage pulses across a fluid. It is highly
effective for pumpable liquids with high dielectric strength (like milk or juices) to
punch holes in bacterial cell membranes.
Q36

(1) 2-3% higher than desired

In a 2-stage system, powder is discharged from the main chamber at

Q37
The difference observed in ice-cream manufactured by low temperature
extrusion (LTE) processing compared to continuous freezer is:
(1) Much smaller size air bubbles
(2) Larger Ice crystal size
(3) Lower percentage of water frozen
(4) Higher drawing temperature
Answer: (1) Much smaller size air bubbles
Explanation
LTE uses intense shear forces at very low temperatures (-12 to -15°C),
which physically pulverizes the air cells and ice crystals into microscopic sizes,
creating a vastly smoother, creamier texture.
Q38
Which of the following is not a natural inherent protective factor present
in raw cow milk?
(1) Lactoferrin
(2) Haemagglutinin
(3) IgG
(4) Lysozyme
Answer: (2) Haemagglutinin
Explanation
Lactoferrin, Immunoglobulins (IgG), and Lysozyme are all natural
antimicrobial/protective proteins in raw milk. Haemagglutinin is typically associated
with viruses (like Influenza) or certain beans, not raw milk.
Q39
Increasing the MSNF content of ice cream does not affect the following
property:
(1) Lowering of freezing point
(2) Increased viscosity
(3) Increased total solids
(4) Enhance the flavouring used
Answer: (4) Enhance the flavouring used
Explanation
Milk Solids-Not-Fat (MSNF) drastically affects freezing point (due to
lactose/salts), increases viscosity, and adds total solids. It does NOT enhance added
flavorings (in fact, excessive MSNF masks flavors).
Q40
Lactose/sucrose crystallization is not a problem in which of the
following frozen product?
(1) Sherbets
(2) Ices
(3) Chocolate ice cream
(4) Soft serve ice cream
Answer: (4) Soft serve ice cream
Explanation
Soft serve ice cream is consumed immediately upon freezing. Without
the prolonged frozen storage and temperature fluctuations (heat shock) of hardened
ice cream, crystallization simply does not have time to occur.

Q42

Q43

Q44
For long transportation of ice cream, which of the following will be most
suited?
(1) Use of ice and salt mixture
(2) Use of liquid nitrogen

prolonged transport of ice cream.

Q46
The desired minimum sugar ratio to be maintained in preparation of
sweetened condensed milk is:
(1) 8.5
(2) 42.5
(3) 62.5
(4) 68.5
Answer: (3) 62.5

A sugar-in-water ratio (Sugar Ratio) of 62.5% to 64.5% is absolutely

Q47
The overrun in ice cream is indicated by:
(1) Ammeter reading in the freezer

Explanation
Overrun (air incorporation) physically reduces the density of the
product. By weighing a precise 1-liter volume of the extruded ice cream and
comparing it to the mix, overrun is instantly calculated.
Q48
Flowability of milk powder can be represented by which of the following

Q49
Sweetened condensed milk contaminated with rennin like enzyme
producing bacterial strain may cause:
(1) Bloating

Answer: (3) Bacterial thickening

Proteolytic enzymes (like rennin) produced by surviving micrococci or

gel or 'bacterial thickening'.
Q50
Contamination of condensed milk with yeast, Torulalactis condensi may
cause:
(1) Bloating
(2) Bacterial thickening
(3) Mould buttons
(4) Souring
Answer: (1) Bloating
Explanation
Osmophilic yeasts like *Torula lactis-condensi* ferment the high
concentrations of sucrose, producing massive amounts of CO2 gas which bloats and
explodes the sealed tin cans.
Q51
Which of the following methods of sealing is a non-contact process that
applies an electromagnetic field to heat seal a container with a screw cap
which has a heat sealable foil inner seal?
(1) Dielectric sealing
(2) Ultrasound sealing
(3) Impulse sealing
(4) Induction heat sealing
Answer: (4) Induction heat sealing
Explanation
Induction sealing uses an oscillating electromagnetic field to instantly
heat the aluminum foil layer inside a plastic cap, melting the polymer coating and
fusing the foil to the bottle lip.
Q52
Which of the following plastic packaging materials of about 10 microns
is commonly used for individually wrapping citrus fruits by blowing hot air
for a tight seal, which delays senescence and restricts spreading of
spoilage?
(1) HDPE
(2) Nylon
(3) PVC
(4) LDPE
Answer: (3) PVC
Explanation
Plasticized Polyvinyl Chloride (PVC) is the classic 'shrink-wrap' film. It
has excellent memory and oxygen permeability tailored to delay respiration
(senescence) in fresh citrus fruits.
Q53
Which of the following combined treatments of aseptic packaging
systems is most suitable for sterilizing packaging materials?
(1) Dipping in peracetic acid and hot air drying
(2) Dipping in H2O2 solution and hot air drying
(3) Irradiation and hot air
(4) Dipping in H2O2 and saturated steam
Answer: (2) Dipping in H2O2 solution and hot air drying
Explanation
The global standard for aseptic cartons (like Tetra Pak) involves
bathing the packaging material in a hot 30-35% Hydrogen Peroxide (H2O2) solution,
followed by extreme hot air to dry it and incinerate any residual H2O2.
Q54
Which of the following gases used in modified atmosphere packaging is
an inert filler gas being more soluble and denser than nitrogen and is able to
remove O2 from the reactive sites more efficiently?
(1) Carbon dioxide
(2) Argon
(3) Carbon monoxide
(4) Ozone
Answer: (2) Argon
Explanation
Argon is a noble gas that is significantly denser than Nitrogen. Its
density allows it to 'blanket' the food, effectively displacing oxygen from microscopic
crevices much better than N2.
Q55

Q56
Read the statements pertaining to modified atmosphere packaging.
Choose the correct answer.
(1) (A), (B) and (D) only
(2) (B) and (D) only
(3) (A) and (C) only
(4) (B), (C) and (D) only
