Q1
The organism commonly associated with infantile diarrhea
Escherichia coli
Enterobacter aerogenes
Salmonella typhi
Clostridium difficile
Answer: 1. Escherichia coli
Explanation
Enteropathogenic *Escherichia coli* (EPEC) is infamous globally for causing severe, epidemic infantile diarrhea, especially in unhygienic environments or contaminated infant formulas.
Q2
Phage resistant medium for the growth of lactic acid bacteria shall NOT contain
Magnesium
Manganese
Calcium
Zinc
Answer: 3. Calcium
Explanation
Bacteriophages absolutely require free Calcium ions to physically attach and inject their viral DNA into the starter cells. Phage Resistant Media (PRM) uses heavily concentrated phosphates/citrates to completely chelate and remove Calcium.
