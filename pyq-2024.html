Q1
Which of the following terms best describe 'odour blindness' in population?
(1) Insomina
(2) Sensory processing disorder
(3) Anosmia
(4) Ageusia
Answer: (3) Anosmia
Explanation
Anosmia is the medical term for the complete loss of the sense of
smell (odour blindness). Ageusia is loss of taste.
Q2
In which of the following sensory evaluation techniques, the task of a
panelist is to indicate a sample which is most similar to the reference product
or sample?
(1) Duo-Trio Test
(2) Paired comparison test
(3) Triangle test
(4) Dual standard test
Answer: (1) Duo-Trio Test
Explanation
In a Duo-Trio test, the panelist is given a reference sample and two
coded samples, and is asked to identify which of the coded samples is identical to
the reference.

Q3
The type of errors made by sensory panelists wherein when a product of
'poor' quality was presented followed by a product of higher quality, the
difference was exaggerated, and the higher quality product was scored much
higher than if it was preceded by a product close in quality is referred to as
(1) Proximity error
(2) Stimulus error
(3) Contrast and convergence error
(4) Time-order error
Answer: (3) Contrast and convergence error
Explanation
This psychological effect is known as the Contrast Error, where
presenting a poor sample directly before a good one artificially inflates the
perceived quality of the good sample.
