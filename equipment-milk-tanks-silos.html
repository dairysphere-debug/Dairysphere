Controlled milk storage

Temperature, residence time, agitation and hygiene all affect product quality.

Insulation

Limits heat gain.

Agitation

Maintains uniformity when required.

CIP

Allows hygienic cleaning of product-contact surfaces.

Main construction features

CIP spray device

5

Level measurement

Supports inventory and automatic sequences.

Vent/protection

Controls gas movement and protects against unsafe pressure/vacuum.
