The simulant used to conduct migration test for milk products is
Ethanol 10 %
Olive oil
Acetic Acid
Ethanol 50 %
Answer: 4. Ethanol 50 %
Explanation
To strictly validate that toxic plastics don't leach into milk, packaging materials must pass extreme 'Migration Tests'. Because milk is an oil-in-water emulsion with some fat-solubility, 50% Ethanol is legally mandated globally as the absolute perfect chemical simulant to mimic milk.
Q155
Water activity of salted butter is
0.91-0.93
0.99-1.00
0.10-0.30
0.77-0.85
Answer: 4. 0.77-0.85
Explanation
While pure water has an aw of 1.0, Butter is a water-in-oil emulsion. Adding 2% salt to the tiny moisture droplets violently spikes their osmotic pressure, massively depressing the vapor pressure and dropping the final Water Activity of salted butter to a highly stable 0.91 to 0.93.
