MILK QUALITY

Lactometer is a screening tool

💧 WaterWater addition generally lowers density and can lower the corrected reading. A low SG is a screening warning, not proof by itself.
🧈 Fat skimmingRemoving fat can increase density and therefore increase the lactometer reading.
🧪 Other solidsAdded dissolved substances can change density and may mask dilution; confirm suspected adulteration with appropriate tests.
🔬 Best practiceUse lactometer reading together with fat, SNF/TS and suitable confirmatory tests.
