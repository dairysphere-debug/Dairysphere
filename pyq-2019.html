2019 Dairy Technology PYQ

160 questions • Answers and explanations in webpage text format.

Q1
Which of the following whey protein is less sensitive to heat treatment at 90°C?
(1) Bovine albumin
(2) Immunoglobulin
(3) a-lactoglobulin
(4) α-lactalbumin
Answer: (4) α-lactalbumin
Explanation
α-lactalbumin is structurally more heat-stable compared to β- lactoglobulin.
Q2
Which of the following chemical is added to control 'blowing' problem caused by butyric acid bacteria in Emmantal cheese?
(1) Potassium nitrite
(2) Potassium nitrate
(3) Hydrogen peroxide
(4) Potassium metabisulphite
Answer: (3) Hydrogen peroxide
Explanation
H2O2 controls late blowing effectively without inhibiting the essential propionic acid bacteria required for eye formation.
Q3
Which of the following milk product has several layers of clotted cream?
(1) Basundi
(2) Rabri
(3) Shrikhand
(4) Paneer
Answer: (2) Rabri
Explanation
Rabri is prepared by continuously skimming off the clotted cream (malai) skin, resulting in a multi-layered texture.
Q4
As per FSSR (2001), the minimum protein content (w/w) in whey powder is
(1) 5%
(2) 7%
(3) 10%
(4) 15%
Answer: (3) 10%
Explanation
According to Food Safety and Standards Regulations, standard whey powder must possess a minimum protein content of 10% by weight.
Q5
The main flavor compound in Dahi is
(1) Acetaldehyde
(2) Diacetyl
(3) Lactones
(4) Alcohols
Answer: (2) Diacetyl
Explanation
Diacetyl (2,3-butanedione) is the principal volatile compound responsible for the buttery and acidic flavor of Dahi.
Q6

(1) Butyric acid
(2) Capric acid
(3) Stearic acid
(4) Oleic acid
Answer: (1) Butyric acid
Explanation

Q7
Body and texture of cheese is contributed by
(1) Proteolysis
(2) Lipolysis
(3) Glycolysis
(4) Hydrolysis
Answer: (1) Proteolysis
Explanation
The breakdown of the casein matrix into smaller peptides (proteolysis) during ripening softens the cheese and develops texture.
Q8

(1) Acetaldehyde
(2) Acetone
(3) Methyl ketone
(4) Ethyl alcohol
Answer: (1) Acetaldehyde

Acetaldehyde is the primary flavor-giving compound in yogurt, produced symbiotically by L. bulgaricus and S. thermophilus.
Q9
Enzymatic phase of rennet action is
(1) Temperature independent
(2) Temperature dependent
(3) Ca++ dependent
(4) Ca++ independent
Answer: (4) Ca++ independent
Explanation
The primary (enzymatic) phase splits κ-casein and is Ca++ independent. The secondary phase requires calcium.
Q10
Rate of evaporation increases as
(1) Exposed surface area of liquid increases
(2) Exposed surface area of liquid decreases
(3) Movement of air above surface of liquid decreases
(4) Atmospheric pressure increases
Answer: (1) Exposed surface area of liquid increases
Explanation
Evaporation is a surface phenomenon. Expanding the surface area directly increases the evaporation rate.

(3) Ultra-filtration

Nano-filtration allows monovalent ions (Na+, Cl-) to pass while retaining lactose, perfect for partial demineralization.
