03 / Balance and feasibility checks

Calculated balance checks will appear here.
Core formulas
Fat mass = Q × F ÷ 100
SNF mass = Q × S ÷ 100
Actual fat = total fat mass ÷ total quantity × 100
Actual SNF = total SNF mass ÷ total quantity × 100
For each target composition, solve ΣQᵢ(Fᵢ − Fₜ) = 0 and ΣQᵢ(Sᵢ − Sₜ) = 0, alongside any selected total-mass equation.
Important: changing a target does not always have a unique solution. Dairion's calculator recalculates Auto quantities only when the selected equations provide enough independent information. If ingredient compositions cannot meet both target fat and SNF, it reports an infeasible balance instead of silently changing the formulas. Litres and kilograms are not interchangeable unless density is known.
