Milk Standardization

Build any standard milk from milk, cream, SMP, WMP, water or other ingredients. Quantities and Fat/SNF are calculated automatically.

Sum of ingredient quantities equals target batch.
Blend fat percentage equals target fat.
Blend SNF percentage equals target SNF.
Fixed quantities are preserved. Auto quantities are solved from active constraints. For multiple valid solutions, the calculator chooses a non-negative solution closest to entered Auto quantities (or an equal starting estimate). Water is prefilled at 0% fat and 0% SNF; verify all other compositions.
