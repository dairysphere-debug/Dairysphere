Formulas used

KG Fat = Quantity × Fat (%) ÷ 100
KG SNF = Quantity × SNF (%) ÷ 100
Actual Fat (%) = Total KG Fat ÷ Total Quantity × 100
Actual SNF (%) = Total KG SNF ÷ Total Quantity × 100

Formula note from your Excel

Some cells in the uploaded workbook multiply quantity directly by the entered fat/SNF number without dividing by 100. For percentage inputs, that gives a result 100 times too high. This simple calculator uses the standard percentage-based mass-balance formulas shown above; it does not reproduce that apparent spreadsheet error.
