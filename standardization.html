Milk Standardization

Build any standard milk from milk, cream, SMP, WMP, water or other ingredients. Quantities and Fat/SNF are calculated automatically.

For two milk variants: enter Milk A and Milk B with their Fat% and SNF%, set one quantity Fixed and the other Auto, then use Mass only to make the required batch and see the resulting Fat/SNF. If both quantities are to be calculated, use Mass + Fat or Mass + SNF. For three Auto ingredients, use Mass + Fat + SNF.
