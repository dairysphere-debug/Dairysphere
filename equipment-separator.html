Cream Separator

A cream separator uses centrifugal force to separate milk into cream and skim milk according to density difference.

Working Principle

A cream separator uses centrifugal force to separate milk into cream and skim milk according to density difference.

Major Components

  • High-speed bowl
  • Disc stack
  • Milk inlet and distribution system
  • Cream and skim outlets
  • Drive motor and safety system

Applications

  • Standardization of milk fat
  • Cream production
  • Skim milk recovery
